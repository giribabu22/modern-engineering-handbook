## Key Ideas

- **There is no single right architecture**: Every company's system reflects its unique constraints, history, and tradeoffs.
- **Scale reveals problems**: What works for 1,000 users breaks at 1 million. At 100 million, everything is a distributed systems problem.
- **Most companies evolve, not design**: The systems we admire today grew organically over years.
- **AI systems are built from familiar parts** *(AI era)*: Behind every AI product are load balancers, caches, queues, databases, and rate limiters — the same building blocks as every system in this section.
