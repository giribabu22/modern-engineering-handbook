### Study tools added to every chapter

Every chapter now includes:

- **An opening quote** from a pioneer of the field, with its source
- **At a Glance** — the chapter in one sentence, what you'll learn, prerequisites, and reading time
- **The Big Picture** — one diagram that shows the whole idea before the details
- **Hands-On Lab** — experiments you can run on your own laptop, tested before publishing
- **Test Yourself** — a quiz with click-to-reveal answers
- **Cheat Sheet** — the key facts on one screen, for revision and interviews
- **In the AI Era** — how the concept applies to AI systems and AI-assisted engineering
- **What to Read Next** — where to go from here
