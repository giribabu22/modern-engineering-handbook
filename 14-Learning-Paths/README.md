# 14 — Learning Paths

> *The handbook is a map. These learning paths are the routes.*

This section provides guided reading paths for different roles and goals. Depending on your experience level and what you want to achieve, different sequences of chapters will be most valuable.

## Learning Paths

| Path | For Whom | Est. Reading Time |
|------|----------|------------------|
| **The Complete Journey** | Anyone who wants to read cover to cover | 20–30 hours |
| **The Beginner's Path** | New engineers (0–2 years) | 10–15 hours |
| **The Backend Path** | Backend engineers looking to deepen their understanding | 15–20 hours |
| **The System Design Path** | Engineers preparing for interviews or designing at scale | 10–15 hours |
| **The SRE / Production Path** | Engineers focused on reliability and operations | 8–12 hours |
| **The Leadership Path** | Senior engineers and tech leads | 5–8 hours |
